FAQ

Questions

How long does a full wrap take?

Timing depends on the vehicle, material, design and preparation. Your confirmed timeframe is provided with your quote.

Can you wrap any vehicle?

Most cars, SUVs, bakkies, motorcycles and commercial vehicles can be considered. We'll confirm the vehicle before quoting.

Can I choose my own colour?

Yes. Tell us the colour and finish you're after and we'll recommend suitable material options.

Do you offer mobile service?

Yes, selected services can be available as a mobile service depending on the job and location.

How do referrals work?

The standard referral reward is 5%, with VIP progression offering up to 10% under the WrapNation referral programme terms.
